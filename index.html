Craft & Material

Woven slowly, worn for years.

Each scarf begins as fine cotton-modal yarn, dyed in small batches and woven on shuttle looms. The check is counted by hand, the fringe twisted one strand at a time, then washed in spring water until the cloth turns soft as dust.
